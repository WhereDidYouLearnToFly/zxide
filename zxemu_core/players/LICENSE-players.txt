Bundled tracker player binaries — third-party
=============================================

pt2_c000.bin
pt3_c000.bin

What they are
-------------
Compiled ZX Spectrum player programs, assembled to load at 0xC000, used to play raw
tracker data (.pt2 / .pt3) that carries no player of its own. zxide loads one into an
emulated machine together with the module and calls it once per frame; see
zxemu_core/sound/tracker_player.py.

Both are builds of the *Universal PT2'n'PT3 Turbo Sound player* by S.V. Bulba
(vorobey@mail.khstu.ru, http://bulba.untergrund.net/), copyright (c) 2004-2007. The two
files differ by a single byte -- the setup value at 0xC00C, 0x02 selecting PT2 and 0x20
selecting PT3 (SETUP.TSPT3) -- and were taken from the "zxstuff" player collection as
found in the ZiFi project sources.

Licence status
--------------
No licence text accompanied either binary or its source in the copies these came from.
Bulba's players have been distributed and reused in ZX Spectrum demos, games and tools
for two decades, and are the de-facto standard for this purpose, but that is a custom
rather than a stated grant.

They are bundled here at the project owner's decision. If terms are later established
that this bundling does not satisfy, removing them is a small change and not a breaking
one: zxide still finds a player next to the project, or via "Find player..." in the Music
Player panel, and every other music format (.ay, and compiled .c modules) carries its own
player and needs nothing from this folder.

covered by it, exactly as the ROM images under zxemu_core/roms/ are not.
